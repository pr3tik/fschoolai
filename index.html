💡 Email Tips
Extensions:
→ Ask 48hrs before due date
→ Give a real reason
→ Most profs say yes once

Grade questions:
→ Never say "I deserved more"
→ Ask "what could I improve"
→ Opens door to re-marking

Absences:
→ Email BEFORE missing
→ Especially for tutorials

Late work:
→ Always ask — most profs accept with penalty
→ Better than a zero
